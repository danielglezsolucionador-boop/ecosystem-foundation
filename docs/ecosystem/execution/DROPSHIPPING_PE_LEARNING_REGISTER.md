## Implementation decisions
- Keep existing generic demo catalogue; select one real hero product and audience after verified quote and sample. Cross-selling only complementary, tested products.
- Use the new supplier checklist as self-declared planning, never as proof or automatic launch authorization. Allow direct local supplier comparison, no platform lock-in.
- Use S/15 per delivered order AND provisional 15% of net sales contribution as scenario targets. Adjustable, not an industry rule; include failed deliveries, payment fees, ad cost and returns reserve. No assumed repeat purchases or organic zero cost.
- Add funnel diagnosis (impressions, clicks, product views, carts, checkout, paid deliveries) and test price/offer sensitivity only when denominator and attribution are consistent. No automated tracking or campaigns yet.
- Measure organic production labor/time, acquisition channel, customer retention and repeat contribution once actual data exist. No fabricated testimonials, fake scarcity, or income promises.
- Preserve brand, existing schema and draft PR; do not procure or activate external services.
